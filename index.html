About this toolkit

Transparent, reproducible reservoir-engineering estimates

Every value here comes from a published, peer-accepted correlation, not a black box. These are screening-level estimates meant to speed up hand calculations, sanity-check simulator output, and teach the underlying physics. For reserves booking or field-development decisions, always calibrate to lab PVT and pressure/production history.

Correlations & references

• Standing, M.B. (1947): Bubble-point pressure, solution GOR, oil FVF.
• Sutton, R.P. (1985): Gas pseudo-critical properties from specific gravity.
• Dranchuk & Abou-Kassem (1975): Gas compressibility factor Z (11-constant EOS fit to Standing–Katz).
• Beggs & Robinson (1975): Dead- & saturated-oil viscosity.
• Lee, González & Eakin (1966): Gas viscosity.
• Arps, J.J. (1945): Decline-curve analysis (exponential, hyperbolic, harmonic).
• Gilbert, W.E. (1954): Critical-flow surface choke correlation, Pwh = 10·qL·R0.546/S1.89.
• Ros (1960), Baxendell (1957), Achong (1961): Same choke form, refitted constants A, B, C.
• Robertson, S. (1988): Modified hyperbolic decline, the terminal-decline switch used for gas wells fit with b > 1.
